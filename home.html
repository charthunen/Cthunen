About · Seattle, WA

A data analyst and brand strategist bridging analytics and creativity.

I recently graduated cum laude from Northeastern University in April 2026 with a B.S. in Data Science and Business Administration, with a concentration in Brand Management. This summer I'm excited to join Point B as a consulting analyst, where I'll be leveraging my quantitative rigor and creative thinking to work on client problems.

I'm a Seattle native, recently returned to the Pacific Northwest after four years on the East Coast. My work lives at the intersection of analytical precision and considered design, I am commited to translating messy data into the kind of clarity that moves people to act.

When I'm not working on a project, you'll find me hiking, training for my next run, exploring coffee shops, or baking sourdough.
